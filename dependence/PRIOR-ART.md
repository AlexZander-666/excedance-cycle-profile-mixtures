# Sources and original contribution

Brenti's class-product formula and conjugacy-class real-rootedness appeared in Theorems 3.1 and 3.2 of his 1993 paper. Each fixed-size example here includes the full cycle and compares nonnegative sums or independently weighted sums of classes. [Brenti, published paper](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1993.157.1)

Alexandersson's question is about consecutive rows of a single upper-tail constraint. The displayed conjecture on SymCat concerns the strict excedance statistic, whereas the separately discussed ordinary-descent problem uses an adjacent comparison of labels. None of the present examples contradicts the long-cycle minimum conjecture. [Original question](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#permutations-with-long-cycles)

An earlier public calculation at size 12 allows only 3-cycles and 4-cycles, giving one nonreal sum of real-rooted class polynomials. At 12 letters that restriction excludes all full cycles and both individual classes have multiple zeros. We credit it explicitly. The size-34 and size-44 shared selections and the size-40 deterministic failure are also in the author's own two disclosed public preliminary reports. [Earlier size-12 example](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/0c07434ef991b853528ca55926b3b83820b7f58d/docs/alexandersson-long-cycle-research.md) · [Size-40 preprint](https://github.com/AlexZander-666/unrestricted-tail-excedance-counterexample/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Mixture preprint](https://github.com/AlexZander-666/excedance-cycle-profile-mixtures/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

The added result holds the one-cycle probabilities fixed and compares the exact weighted permutation row for independent choices with the common-draw row. Its two positive independent-choice certificates and original-row formal contrast are new to the consolidated journal article. We also prove the lower size-39 real-root count in the proof assistant. At 44 the uniform short weight is 1/2; the deterministic full-cycle correction distinguishes it from weighting all derangement cycles by 1/2. Cycle-count-refined Eulerian and derangement enumerators are established objects. [Ma, publisher source](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972711002760)

The targeted search on 26 November 2026 for cycle-weighted derangement and excedance polynomials, restrictions with all long lengths admitted, convex combinations and dependence with matched marginals did not identify a theorem giving the matched comparison for these two source profiles. Direct mathematical and bibliographic checks cover the cited primary sources and the earlier public records. This is a bounded search, not a priority claim.
