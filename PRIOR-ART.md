# Scope and prior work

The examples concern a positive linear combination of two whole strict-excedance endpoint polynomials. Each of the four endpoints has a simple zero and n−2 simple negative roots and contains all cycles of full length. Size 34 takes a 1:2 mixture, and size 44 takes the arithmetic mean of two profiles complementary below the cutoff. In each case, independently excluding any full cycles would change the objects.
 
Alexandersson asks about weak interlacing of hard minimum-length rows as n varies with k fixed. The separate ordinary-descent question changes the permutation statistic. This new failure pertains to an extension investigated by this project. [Original source](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#permutations-with-long-cycles)

Brenti's factorization and single conjugacy-class theorem underlie the cycle enumeration. An earlier public example permits only lengths 3 and 4 at n=12. Its polynomial is the sum of the two individual class polynomials for types 3⁴ and 4³. Brenti's theorem splits both summands, while the earlier calculation exhibits a nonreal zero of their sum. Their supports have different vanishing orders at the origin and omit the 12-cycle. The sharper constraints in these examples are that every length from the admissible cutoff onward is allowed in both endpoint models and that the endpoint zeros are simple. [Brenti, Theorems 3.1–3.2 (1993)](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1993.157.1) · [Earlier explicit example](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/0c07434ef991b853528ca55926b3b83820b7f58d/docs/alexandersson-long-cycle-research.md)

The same earlier file displays a signed combination for different hard thresholds at n=8, which does not furnish a convex-mixture witness. ProgramComputer's estimates establish positive interlacing results for hard cutoffs.
